5.32pm Rohit Sharma after his 162: "Double hundred is a lot of expectations (laughs). We wanted to post a good target because a batting line-up like West Indies play unpredictable cricket so you never know what a good score is. CCI the outfield is good the wicket is good, so I don't know what par score is. Rayudu and myself created a big partnership.

I've grown up playing cricket in Mumbai and more so in CCI. I understand the pace and bounce here and you should use it to your advantage. Once you do that you get value for yours shots. I was trying to not hit it too hard, just maintain my shape throughout my innings.

I generally feel that once you cross 100 it's you making a mistake and getting out. The bowlers will not get you out - you are set, you are seeing the ball nicely, the field nicely. I kept telling myself that the pitch is good, not to play extravagant shots. It's about seeing how far I can go and the team gets into a good position.
