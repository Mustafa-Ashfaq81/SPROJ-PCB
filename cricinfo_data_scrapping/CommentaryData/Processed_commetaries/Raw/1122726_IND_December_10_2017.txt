10.25am Morning, everyone. After the smoggy haze of Delhi, we've moved to the clear, crisp mountain air of Dharamsala, and the first of three ODIs. Usually, limited-overs games at the end of difficult away Test series offer teams a shot at winning a few games and competing on a much more even footing, but in Sri Lanka's case, they come into this short series on a bit of a horror run in ODIs, having lost their last 12 games in the format. They will, however, be up against a slightly unfamiliar-looking India line-up, which is without Virat Kohli, who has been rested, and will instead be led by Rohit Sharma. This should be fun to watch.

Sri Lanka have a new captain too, with Thisara Perera taking over from Upul Tharanga. It's the latest in a long line of captaincy changes they have had over the last few months, forced or voluntary, and they will hope it can turn things around for them in limited-overs cricket.
