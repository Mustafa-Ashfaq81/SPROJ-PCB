Shakeel shifted gears to make the early wicket losses feel like distant memories. They put up 120 and scored 68 each but Dutt got Shakeel on the sweep and wickets were regular from thereon. Shaheen and Haris made 29 among them to push Pakistan late in the innings. De Leede's four-for ensured Netherlands got key middle- and lower-middle order wickets.
