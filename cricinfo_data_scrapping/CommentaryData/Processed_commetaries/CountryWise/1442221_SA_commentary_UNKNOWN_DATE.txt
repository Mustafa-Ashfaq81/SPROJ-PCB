1.10pm After being asked to bat, South Africa have posted 304 for 6 on the back of a record-breaking innings from Breetzke. He marked his ODI debut with 150 off 148 balls. "It hasn't sunk in yet and I'm a bit tired," he tells the host broadcaster. "Hope we can get the win. That's the only thing on my mind. The pitch was tacky and O'Rourke was really tough to face. My mindset was to bat as long as possible and give myself a chance at the end. If we can strike with the ball in the powerplay and squeeze in the middle, we might have enough." Ashish Pant will be back shortly for NZ's chase.
