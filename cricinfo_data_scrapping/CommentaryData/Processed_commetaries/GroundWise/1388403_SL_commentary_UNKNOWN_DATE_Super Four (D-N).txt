6.45pm A whirlwind knock from Samarawickrama, who raced away to a career best 72-ball 93 has helped Sri Lanka power through to 257. It seemed to be a slow pitch and even though it was the Bangladesh pacers who did all the damage, it was hard to get the spinners away. Taskin Ahmed and Hasan Mahmud took three each, while Shoriful chipped in with two crucial wickets in consecutive overs to get rid of the well set Nissanka and Kusal Mendis (who also scored a fifty). Sri Lanka looked in trouble at that point, but Samarawickrama's knock ensured Sri Lanka go into the break with the momentum. Join Madushka in around 20 minutes for the chase!
