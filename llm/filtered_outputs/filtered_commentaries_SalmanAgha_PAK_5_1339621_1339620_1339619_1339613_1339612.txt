Commentary 1:
[CONDITION] Pakistan scraped their way to a somewhat respectable total of 287. The pitch was two-paced and rewarded pacers for sticking to tight lines and lengths and it wasn't easy to get underneath the ball when they took the pace off the ball. Imam-ul-Haq's 90 and another half-century from Babar Azam provided the platform for the likes of **Agha**, Shafique and Rizwan to tee off but credit to New Zealand's bowling attack for putting up an impressive show with the ball towards the death, it was a concerted bowling effort Fielding was spot on from the visitors and the debutant McConchie didn't show any sign of nerves. With Shaheen Afridi and Shadab Khan back in the side, Pakistan's bowling attack looks strong enough to make it a tough chase for New Zealand.
