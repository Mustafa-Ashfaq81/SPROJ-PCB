[DISMISSAL] 6.20pm A comprehensive win for Afghanistan then, as they take a 1-0 lead in this three-match series! It was a victory setup by a classy run-a-ball 98 from Ibrahim **Zadran**, who was unfortunate to miss out on yet another ODI century. His 146-run second wicket stand with Rahmat Shah broke the chase open, and by the time he fell at the end of the 30th over, the required rate was at a very manageable five an over. Sri Lanka's bowlers toiled, but rarely managed to put pressure on the Afghan batters. In the field too Afghanistan showed discipline, and only really looked under the pump when Charith Asalanka (91) and Dhananjaya de Silva (51) were going strong. 268 was a challenging total, but Sri Lanka will know they missed an opportunity to put up an imposing one, losing wickets at crucial junctures. Game two is on Sunday, and the hosts have their work cut out for them.

Commentary 5:
6.20pm So, Afghanistan end up on 313/8, which is well above par, and in fact the third highest total at this ground. All that was of course thanks to Ibrahim **Zadran**, who brought up his third ODI century, his second of this series, and in the process went on to score the first score over 150 by an Afghanistan batter. Sri Lanka simply had no answer to Ibrahim's low risk, high impact innings, which rescued his side from 57 for 3 in the 15th over - a point in the game when Sri Lanka were well on top. But Ibrahim, along with his namesake Najibullah, then proceeded to put on 154 off just 141 balls for the fourth wicket, to take the game away. The Lankan bowlers for their part lacked control in the middle overs after some disciplined bowling from Kasun Rajitha had given them the upper hand early on. Suffice to say, this score will certainly take some getting. Shashwat will be with you for the start of the chase in a bit.
